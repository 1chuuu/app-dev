# 🌟 My Favorite Series: *Young Sheldon*

![Young Sheldon Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/3/3e/Young_Sheldon_season_1.jpg)

**Young Sheldon** is an American coming-of-age sitcom created by *Chuck Lorre* and *Steven Molaro*.  
It serves as a **prequel** to the hit show *The Big Bang Theory* and follows the early life of **Sheldon Cooper**, a child prodigy growing up in East Texas.

---

## 🧠 Plot Summary

Set in the late 1980s and early 1990s, the series explores Sheldon’s childhood as a socially awkward but incredibly intelligent boy who struggles to fit in with his family and classmates.  

> "When you’re a genius, it’s hard to blend in with the rest of the world."

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Main Characters

- **Sheldon Cooper** — Played by *Iain Armitage*  
- **Mary Cooper** — Sheldon’s loving but overprotective mother  
- **George Cooper Sr.** — His father, a high school football coach  
- **Missy Cooper** — Sheldon’s witty twin sister  
- **Georgie Cooper** — His older brother  
- **Meemaw** — Sheldon’s fun and rebellious grandmother  

---

## 💡 Why I Love It

1. It’s **funny**, **heartwarming**, and **relatable**.  
2. It gives great insight into how Sheldon became who he is in *The Big Bang Theory*.  
3. The family dynamic feels genuine and touching.  

---

**Season 2, Episode 22 — "A Swedish Science Thing and the Equation for Toast"**
